# 6.26 Crypto Postmortem Checklist

Use this checklist after each crypto incident or tabletop drill.

## 1) Incident summary

- [ ] Incident ID, severity, and timeline are complete.
- [ ] Root cause statement is explicit and testable.
- [ ] Impact is quantified by environment, write paths, and duration.

## 2) Policy and control-plane validation

- [ ] Decisions align with `docs/security/6.25-crypto-policy-matrix.md`.
- [ ] Any emergency gate change is recorded (`CRYPTO_ROLLOUT_*` values and times).
- [ ] No emergency decision weakened production policy without explicit approval.

## 3) Containment and recovery quality

- [ ] Time-to-containment met SLA target.
- [ ] Recovery steps followed the approved runbook order.
- [ ] If transport risk was involved, `6.19` rollback guardrails were followed.
- [ ] If key risk was involved, rotation/rewrap sequencing was validated.

## 4) Communication quality

- [ ] Internal updates were sent on schedule.
- [ ] External updates (if required) were approved and delivered on SLA.
- [ ] Messages avoided sensitive crypto internals and secret exposure.

## 5) Evidence completeness

- [ ] Metric snapshots and logs are attached.
- [ ] Incident template is fully populated.
- [ ] Go/no-go and final sign-offs are recorded.

## 6) Corrective actions

- [ ] Action items are prioritized with owners and due dates.
- [ ] Monitoring/alerting gaps were converted into tracked tasks.
- [ ] Documentation updates are linked and merged.
- [ ] Learning points are reflected in `6.26` runbook revision notes.

## 7) Closure gate

- [ ] Security lead approved closure.
- [ ] Release owner approved closure.
- [ ] Postmortem published within SLA.
